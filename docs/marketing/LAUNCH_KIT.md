## 📝 2. Textos Prontos para Postagem (Copywriting)

### A. Hacker News — "Show HN"
**Título:**
```text
Show HN: Delayo – Open-source Chrome extension to snooze tabs and boost focus
```

**Texto do Post:**
```markdown
Hi HN! 👋

Like many of you, I suffered from "tab overload syndrome" — dozens of open tabs eating gigabytes of RAM, keeping my browser sluggish and breaking my focus. Most tab managers either convert everything into static bookmarks you never look at again, or collect telemetry.

I built **Delayo** (MIT open-source): a lightweight, privacy-first Chrome extension that lets you "snooze" tabs and have them automatically reopen exactly when you need them.

Key things we built into v1.2.0:
- ⚡ **Turbo Action (+1h)**: One-click delay when you need an immediate 1-hour focus sprint.
- 🕒 **Context-Aware Dynamic Presets**: Labels and scheduling that adapt dynamically (e.g. shifts automatically if the evening deadline passed).
- 📅 **Native Integrated Calendar & TimePicker**: Clean, custom scheduling without ugly browser dialogs.
- 🔒 **100% Privacy & Local Insights**: Zero tracking or external telemetry; stats and queues stay purely in local browser storage.
- 🛠️ **Tech stack**: React 19, TypeScript 5, Vite 5, Tailwind CSS & DaisyUI.

GitHub: https://github.com/allud1t/Delayo
Chrome Web Store: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/delayo

I’d love to hear your feedback, feature ideas, and suggestions!
```

```text
How I solved my "50 open tabs anxiety" — built a free, privacy-first tab snoozer (Delayo)
```

**Texto:**
```markdown
Hey everyone!

I used to keep 30–50 tabs open "just in case I need them later today or tomorrow". The result?
1. Constant visual distraction.
2. My laptop's fans spinning like a jet engine.
3. Feeling overwhelmed every time I opened Chrome.

Bookmarking didn't work because bookmarks go to a digital graveyard.

So I created **Delayo**, a free and open-source Chrome extension that temporarily puts your tabs to sleep and wakes them up automatically at the scheduled time (Later today, Tonight, Tomorrow, Weekend, or custom recurring intervals).

Features I find most useful:
- **⚡ Turbo (+1h)**: Immediately snoozes active or selected tabs for 1 hour with a single click.
- **Smart Recurring Delays**: Schedule timesheet portals or daily dashboards to open automatically on weekdays.
- **Zero data collection**: No accounts, no ads, no trackers.

It's completely free and open-source on GitHub. If you struggle with tab hoarding, give it a try!

🔗 GitHub: https://github.com/allud1t/Delayo
```
