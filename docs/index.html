Your tabs do not need to be right now.

Delayo is a free, open-source Chrome extension that lets you postpone tabs and bring them back when the moment is right. Keep the useful page. Lose the visual noise.

No account inside Delayo. Delayed tabs and settings stay local in the browser.

Make room for the work in front of you.

Bookmarks are for things you may need someday. Delayo is for the page you already know you want, just not in this moment.

A tiny tool for a very familiar problem.

Delayo keeps the interaction quick, visible, and easy to undo. It is made for articles, research, comparisons, and the pages that should not become permanent furniture.
